# Describe work at capability level

The site and the public résumé describe roles by what I built and which problems I solved, without employer or product names. LinkedIn carries those. Security mechanisms, numeric thresholds, incidents and unreleased features stay out entirely: recruiters don't need them, and they aren't mine to publish.

Bullets carry numbers only when I have real, measured ones. Until then they describe outcome and approach. The same rule applies to the "How I build with Claude Code" section: no productivity multipliers unless I say how they were measured.
